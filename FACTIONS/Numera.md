# The Numerated Flock
Deified Dictatorship of Cyber-Integrationists and Daemons building a utopia under [[TIMEKEEPER]]

Naming Conventions:
Names inspired by youth subculture usernames of the old Munin Network. Usually two words, sometimes more, with a particular word (often but not always the last) being more commonly used as a shorter personal name. It's not uncommon for the name to be chosen, rather than given, and Numerans often tweak it or change it to suit major life changes.
