# Mech: Azulifer

Azulifer is a lightweight mech designed for speed and aerial acrobatics. It is bipedal and vaguely humanoid, comparable in appearance to a mechanical knight. It wields an enormous sniper rifle. Two long, trailing ribbons sprout from Azulifer’s temples and drape far below its knees.
