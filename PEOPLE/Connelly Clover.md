Nelly started her career as a heavy industry pilot. She took to the controls of HUGE machines with incredible finesse and began to show off with flourishes during her work that happened to catch the eye of an Ephraim agent who thought she could make it as a Hero of Ares. She leans into her rural upbringing with a twanging drawl and streak of small town pride.

The biggest single pilot combat mech ever made, the Titan was originally built as heavy terraforming machinery, but refitted to have all the needed controls within reach of a single pilot.
