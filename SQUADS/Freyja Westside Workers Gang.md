Blue Collar SarCo Squad in Freyja
The Freyja Westside Worker's Gang started as a loose union of dock workers, construction workers, etc that eventually became its own company.
They're incredibly blue collar in makeup and attitude, and are also assigned to Add Franter (they're Franter's biggest client). The Westside Workers are much more likely to be called on for and take jobs that are more about cargo loading, construction, etc and most of their mechs are geared for such. 
They have a positive opinion of Argent Logistics because they like that we're locals, and they like that we do "real work".
## Persons of Interest
**Roman Simkin**
-# **He/Him** Westside Dry Dock Owner
Roman is a huge man; tall, brawny, and a bit pudgy. He has bright blue eyes, a bald head, and a grizzled and graying beard. Roman is almost always jolly and helpful in the presence of friends, family, or customers, and is always doting on his stepdaughter, Xiaohua. He was fond of Avalyn and talks her ear off whenever he spots her at the dry dock, gently but regularly "joking" that she should go on another date with his daughter.
**Xiaohua Li**
-# **She/Her** Firebrand Mechanic
Stepdaughter of Roman Simkin, ex-girlfriend of Avalyn, and one of the mechanics of the Westside Dry Docks, Xiaohua is a compassionate but fiercely frank and opinionated young woman. She speaks loudly and honestly to all that meet her without regard for being delicate nor polite, but is quick to lend help, advice, and companionship to anyone she considers a good person.
